# Turek–Hron FSI1 Technical Report

## 1. Problem and Objective

Reproduce the fluid–structure interaction benchmark **FSI1** (steady laminar
flow, Re = 20) introduced by Hron & Turek (ECCOMAS CFD 2006). The geometry is a
2D channel with a fixed rigid cylinder and an elastic beam attached behind it;
the fluid loads the beam, whose deformation in turn changes the flow field,
forming two-way coupling. The objective is to compute the beam-tip displacement
(point A = (0.6, 0.2) m) and the drag/lift on the cylinder-plus-beam, then
compare them with the published reference values.

## 2. Geometry and Mesh

- Domain: 2.5 m long × 0.41 m high;
- Cylinder: centre (0.2, 0.2) m, radius 0.05 m;
- Elastic beam: 0.35 m × 0.02 m, fixed at x = 0.25 m, free tip at x = 0.6 m;
- Fluid–structure interface: the beam and cylinder surfaces.

The fluid mesh is generated by `blockMesh` (`fluid/system/blockMeshDict`): 20
hexahedral blocks, one cell thick in 2D (`empty` front/back), **6306** cells in
total. The structural mesh `solid/turekflap.msh` is 3D, extruded 0.01 m in z,
with quadratic reduced-integration elements **C3D20R** and 910 nodes.

## 3. Governing Equations and Discretization

The fluid is the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations in ALE (moving-mesh)
form, solved with OpenFOAM `pimpleFoam`; first-order Euler time scheme,
`nCorrectors=2`, `nNonOrthogonalCorrectors=1`. The structure is solved with
CalculiX as large-deformation dynamics (`*DYNAMIC, DIRECT`, implicit
Newton–Raphson, geometric nonlinearity `NLGEOM` enabled).

## 4. Physical Parameters

FSI1 (the subject of this report):

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Fluid density ρ_f | 1000 kg/m³ |
| Kinematic viscosity ν_f | 0.001 m²/s |
| Mean inlet velocity U | 0.2 m/s (parabolic profile, 0–2 s cosine ramp) |
| Reynolds number Re = U·D/ν | 20 (D = 0.1 m) |
| Solid density ρ_s | 1000 kg/m³ |
| Young's modulus E | 1.4 MPa |
| Poisson's ratio ν_s | 0.4 |
| Time step Δt | 0.01 s |
| Duration T | 20 s (~2000 coupling windows) |

FSI3 (configuration prepared, short trial runs passed, full run pending):
U = 2.0 m/s (Re = 200), E = 5.6 MPa, Δt = 0.0005 s.

## 5. Coupling Method

Partitioned coupling via **preCICE** with a `serial-implicit` scheme:

- Within each time window, fluid and solid solve and exchange data in turn until
  convergence;
- Data exchange: fluid → solid sends **Force**, solid → fluid sends
  **Displacement**;
- Acceleration: **IQN-ILS** quasi-Newton with initial relaxation 0.1, reusing
  10 previous time windows;
- Mapping: global RBF direct mapping (thin-plate splines), `conservative` for
  forces and `consistent` for displacements;
- Convergence: displacement `abs-limit=1e-8, rel-limit=1e-6` (strict) and force
  `rel-limit=1e-2` (strict), at most 100 iterations per window.

The OpenFOAM side connects through the `preciceAdapterFunctionObject` adapter;
the CalculiX side through the `ccx_preCICE` binary produced by the
calculix-adapter.

## 6. Engineering Issues and Fixes

1. **preCICE v2 → v3 configuration syntax**: the original case targets preCICE
   v2. The XML was rewritten: removed `solver-interface`, declared mesh
   dimensions explicitly, used `provide-mesh`/`receive-mesh`, socket
   `acceptor`/`connector`, and the new RBF-mapping syntax.
2. **groovyBC dependency**: the original case uses groovyBC for the inlet, which
   needs an extra shared library. Replaced with the native `codedFixedValue`
   parabolic inlet from the official mainline tutorial, dropping the groovyBC
   dependency.
3. **Wrong total-force monitoring density**: the `forces` function object used
   `rhoInf = 1`; corrected to 1000 (consistent with the coupling), and the 2D
   unit-thickness force is divided by 0.01 m to obtain N/m.
4. **Overly strict displacement criterion**: the PR's `abs-limit=1e-10` failed
   to converge within 100 iterations at dt = 0.01 s (`results/runs/fsi1-pilot`
   aborted on the strict criterion). Relaxed the displacement `abs-limit` to
   `1e-8` and tightened the fluid linear-solver tolerances (p 1e-8, U 1e-9,
   `relTol=1e-5`); `fsi1-pilot-tuned` then converged stably.

## 7. Results and Reference Comparison

The complete 20 s run has 2000 coupling windows, **all converged**
(`unconverged_windows = 0`), with a mean of 2.06 and a maximum of 4 coupling
iterations per window, indicating healthy IQN-ILS convergence. Steady-state
values are averaged over the final 2 s and compared with the Hron & Turek 2006
reference:

| Quantity | Simulation | Reference | Error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tip x-displacement ux | 2.198e-5 m | 2.27e-5 m | −3.2 % |
| Tip y-displacement uy | 8.132e-4 m | 8.209e-4 m | −0.94 % |
| Drag | 14.253 N/m | 14.295 N/m | −0.29 % |
| Lift | 0.7592 N/m | 0.7638 N/m | −0.60 % |

Over the final 2 s the displacement peak-to-peak is about 1e-9 m and the force
peak-to-peak about 2e-4 N/m, confirming steady state. Displacement and force
errors are within about 3 %, with force errors below 1 %. Forces are obtained by
integrating the total (pressure + viscous) force on the cylinder and beam with
the OpenFOAM `forces` function object, divided by the 0.01 m thickness to yield
N/m.

## 8. Limitations

- This reproduction demonstrates that the OpenFOAM + CalculiX + preCICE
  partitioned-coupling pipeline runs stably and produces results within about
  3 % of the published reference, completing the full
  modelling → coupled solve → post-processing → reference-comparison loop.
- No mesh-independence or time-step-independence study has been performed; the
  geometry and mesh are reused from a third-party PR; the FSI3 full computation
  has not been completed. The conclusion is therefore "numerically close to the
  published reference", not a formal verification.
